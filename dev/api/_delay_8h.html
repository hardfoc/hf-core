Detailed Description

hf::DelayUs() and hf::DelayMs(): the delays used above the platform bind.

standards/threading-and-timing.md: a delay of 1 ms or more blocks the task through rtos-wrap's os_delay_msec(), rounded up to whole milliseconds so a datasheet minimum of 2.5 ms never becomes 2 ms; a shorter one busy-waits on hf_time::MonotonicUs() and makes no RTOS call. Nothing here tests an MCU macro: the MCU-specific parts are in hf_time and rtos-wrap.

Busy-waits longer than about 100 us should be avoided in tasks above idle priority.
